    Best experienced with sound

    Vossberg V9 Aquila XXI Coupé

    Haarlem 07:00

    The morning

    Scroll to explore

    I

    Carmine lacquer

    Colour with depth

    Fourteen coats of Carmine, each rubbed back by hand, give the body a deep and quiet glow. Bronze trim traces every shut-line, so the shape reveals itself as the light moves.

    II

    wheel design

    Turning, standing still

    Fine radial spokes suggest motion before the wheel has moved an inch. At the centre sits the family motto: Fortes Fortuna Adiuvat.

    III

    lamp design

    Eyes that glow

    The headlamps sit deep behind bronze lattice. Each unit is hand-assembled, so the light reads warm and slightly soft, like an old cinema lens.

    IV

    The lattice

    Structure into signature

    Borrowed from naval architecture, the lattice delivers strength from very little material. Layered and re-cut, it gives the grille its depth and returns throughout the car as the Vossberg motif.

    V

    Fins & flaps

    Shaped by the wind

    Small bronze fins set into the sills take their cue from the trim tabs of a sailing yacht. Precise and modest, they sharpen the profile without breaking its line.

    Roof scoop & exhaust

    Breathe in. Roar out.

    A central roof scoop feeds cold air straight to the 780 hp twin-turbo V8. Stacked exhausts give the engine the shortest possible path from combustion to open air.

    Bred on the circuitTamed for the road

    Road and track have never been separate worlds at Vossberg. The same engineers, the same methods and the same stubbornness move between them. Racing puts every decision under pressure: cooling, balance, braking, durability, control. What we learn at Spa and Zandvoort finds its way into the road car.

    The Vossberg V9 Aquila XXI Coupé carries that exchange forward: built for the road, yet shaped by the same instinct that has always driven Vossberg on the circuit.

    Ardennes 14:00

    The afternoon

    Scroll to explore

    Performance

    Horsepower

    780HP

    Compact, light and generous with torque, the 4.2-litre twin-turbo V8 answers instantly on road and track.

    Torque

    820NM

    Every change begins and ends with your hand. Mechanical, deliberate and tactile, the six-speed gearbox keeps the driver at the centre of the car.

    Max RPM

    8200RPM

    It never needs to be pushed. As revs climb, the roof scoop feeds the V8 and the stacked exhausts carry its voice into the open.

    Dry weight

    1410KG

    Six-piston bronze-finished calipers and carbon-ceramic discs bring speed back under control with precision and calm.

    0-100 KM/H

    2,9SECONDS

    A purposeful rake and carefully resolved aerodynamics guide air over, through and under the body, building stability as speed rises.

    Antwerp 22:00

    The evening

    Scroll to explore

    I

    Dashboard & dials

    Instruments, not screens

    Ship-inspired dials glow amber against engine-turned aluminium. Real switches keep every function visible, immediate and worked by hand.

    II

    Leather quilting

    The pattern comes inside

    The lattice passes from the exterior mesh into the cabin, quilted and double-stitched into deep, soft leather. One geometry, carried from structure to touch.

    III

    Manual gearbox

    The mechanical ritual

    The open-gate lever and bronze surround are shaped and finished like a fine instrument. Every shift stays deliberate, tactile and entirely yours.

    IV

    Steering wheel

    Everything, at hand

    A slim wooden rim places the essential controls exactly where your hands already are.
